04

Colour blocks

Big flat fields, no shadows, no texture. Product screens use at most two accents at once; this reference shows all four for inspection.

Accent 01 / MaroonVol. 01

Big, flat, unapologetic.

White on maroon is 11.75:1. Large surfaces earn the colour; type stays quiet and the edge does the work.

Back to colour
Accent 02 / VermilionVol. 02

Loud, but legible.

Black on vermilion is 5.23:1. Ink is 4.33:1 and fails normal text, so this block uses black for small copy.

Back to colour
Accent 03 / CobaltVol. 03

A cold, clean interruption.

White on cobalt is 7.19:1. Use it for a single structural section, not as a second background for the whole page.

Back to colour
Accent 04 / ButterVol. 04

A warm punctuation mark.

Ink on butter is 10.95:1. Keep it for highlights and small blocks; white on butter disappears.

Back to colour
